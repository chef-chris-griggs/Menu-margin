Recipe costing

Build a recipe, check the cost per portion and price it to hit your target GP.

Recipe library

Saved recipes sync to your signed-in account. Use Export CSV to keep a portable backup of your recipe.

To edit a saved recipe, choose it here and tap Open, then return to Cost a recipe. Your changes are not saved until you tap Save recipe.

Ingredient price book

For each ingredient, enter the quantity used in the recipe, the total size of the pack you buy, and the full pack price. Choose the matching units.

Price book

Invoice case quantities and individual item sizes are shown separately (e.g. 6 × 850 g). The case price is divided across the full contents for recipe costing. Pack sizes are converted to grams or millilitres for recipes: a 2 kg pack is stored as 2,000 g. For each/countable items, use each. Saved prices are copied into recipes, so changing a supplier price won't silently change existing recipe costs.

Invoice scanner

Upload a supplier PDF or photograph, extract text, review the prices and import only what you approve.

Beta
Choose a PDF or photo to begin. Processing happens in your browser using external OCR libraries.

Invoice layouts vary, so OCR may miss or misread product descriptions, pack sizes, discounts or prices. Always check the review list against the original invoice. This beta does not read invoice totals as a substitute for checking each line, and it does not save anything until you import selected rows.

Review detected products

Edit each row. Amber fields are missing or conflicting OCR values. Fill in the pack size and price, then tick only products you want to add. The supplier is taken from the field above.
