Before you import

Check the supplier pack, not just the price.

A supplier invoice can contain cases, individual packs and different purchase units. Before saving extracted information, compare the product description, quantity, pack size, unit and price against the original invoice. A case of six tins is not the same costing unit as one tin, even when the product name looks identical.

For example, if a case contains six 850 g packs, the total purchased weight is 5,100 g. Entering 850 g instead of the full case weight against the case price would overstate the ingredient cost. Check VAT treatment too, so the prices in your recipes use the same VAT basis as your ex-VAT selling prices.

Once you've approved the entries, keep them in your supplier price book and review any affected recipe costings when buying prices change.
