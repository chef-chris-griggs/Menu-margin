THE FORMULA

How to calculate food gross profit

GP per portion = selling price excluding VAT − ingredient cost per portion.

Food-cost GP % = (GP per portion ÷ selling price excluding VAT) × 100.

EXAMPLE(12.00 − 3.60) ÷ 12.00 × 100= 70% GP

Food-cost GP is based on ingredient costs only. Labour, energy, rent, overheads and waste are not automatically deducted. Compare ingredient costs and selling prices on a consistent VAT basis.

GP vs food cost

Food cost percentage is ingredient cost divided by selling price. With a 30% food cost, ingredient-based GP is 70%.

£

Price for target GP

Divide ingredient cost per portion by (1 − target GP decimal). £3 cost at 75% target GP gives a £12 ex-VAT selling price.
