Website privacy and cookies

Updated 8 November 2026.

Optional website analytics

If you choose Accept analytics, this public website loads Google Analytics through our Google tag. Google receives visit information such as pages visited, browser and device information, approximate location, referral source and interactions. Analytics cookies help distinguish visits. We use the reports to understand which marketing channels bring visitors and whether people use the free calculator, download our template or click through to the app.

Our website events do not include your recipe name, ingredient entries, recipe costs, email address, account ID or invoice contents. The website tag is not added to the signed-in application. App-link clicks are not confirmation that someone registered for a trial or bought a subscription.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you accept. Rejecting analytics keeps the Google tag blocked and does not prevent you from using the website or calculator. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to change your choice. When you withdraw consent we disable analytics, remove accessible Google Analytics cookies on this domain and reload the page to stop the running tag.

This browser remembers your choice for up to 180 days using the local-storage item mep-analytics-consent-v1. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies to the current page. When you accept, Google may create cookies with names beginning _ga, configured here for up to 180 days. Browser settings can also clear website storage.

Google and hosting

Google processes analytics information under its own policies and service terms. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages; the host receives network information needed to serve page requests. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation are denied in our website consent settings.

Accounts, saved recipes and support

For the full application’s accounts, recipes, supplier prices, invoice handling and data requests, read the application privacy notice. For privacy questions, use our help and contact page.
