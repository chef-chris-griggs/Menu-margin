A practical costing check

What does each portion really cost?

Start with a recipe you already make. For every ingredient, record the quantity used and the price of the purchased pack in matching units. Add those ingredient costs to calculate your batch total, then divide by the number of saleable portions. Check that your yield is realistic: a twenty-portion batch only costs what you expect if it actually serves twenty portions.

For example, a soup batch with £18 in ingredient costs and ten portions has an ingredient cost of £1.80 per portion. At a £6 selling price excluding VAT, that is £4.20 ingredient-based gross profit per portion, or 70% food GP. It is not net profit: staffing, utilities, overheads and waste still matter.
