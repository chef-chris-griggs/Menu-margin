How to calculate your recipe cost

  1. Add your ingredients. Enter the quantity used, purchased pack size and price for the whole pack. Keep units compatible within each ingredient.
  2. Set your portions. Enter how many portions the full batch makes.
  3. Add your selling price. Use a price excluding VAT when checking food-cost GP.
  4. Review the result. The calculator shows total ingredient cost, cost per portion, cash GP per portion and food-cost GP percentage.

Worked example: recipe costing and GP

Suppose a batch of soup costs £12.00 in ingredients and makes 10 portions. You sell each portion for £5.00 excluding VAT.

Total ingredient cost£12.00
Portions made10
Ingredient cost per portion£1.20
Selling price per portion (ex VAT)£5.00
Ingredient-based GP per portion£3.80
Ingredient-based GP %76%

Cost per portion = total batch ingredient cost ÷ portions made. Ingredient-based GP % = (selling price excluding VAT − ingredient cost per portion) ÷ selling price excluding VAT × 100.

This is an ingredient-only gross profit calculation, not net profit. It does not automatically account for labour, utilities, rent, wastage or other operating costs. Compare costs and selling prices on a consistent VAT basis.

Recipe costing questions

Is the recipe calculator free?

Yes. The one-recipe calculator on our homepage can be used without signing in or entering payment details.

Can I calculate the food cost percentage?

Yes. Food cost percentage is the ingredient cost per portion divided by the selling price, both on a consistent VAT basis, multiplied by 100. Ingredient-based GP % is 100 minus that food cost percentage.

What happens when supplier prices change?

Re-entering revised pack prices shows how the cost of a dish changes. The full Mise en Price app also provides a supplier price book and linked recipe pricing, which can make repeated re-costing easier.
