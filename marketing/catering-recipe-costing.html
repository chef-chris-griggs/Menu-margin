How do I calculate catering food cost per person?

Add up the ingredient cost of all planned dishes and divide by the number of covers that batch is intended to serve. Check the yield of each dish and whether the portion estimates are realistic. The result is an ingredient cost per head, not the total cost to deliver the event.

Example: a buffet for 40 guests

If the ingredients for the complete buffet cost £160 and the menu is planned for 40 guests, the average ingredient cost is £4.00 per person.

Estimated covers40
Total ingredient cost£160
Ingredient cost per head£4.00
Illustrative selling price per head (ex VAT)£16.00
Ingredient-based GP75%

This GP ignores staffing, equipment, serving supplies, deliveries, venue costs, waste and other overheads. The example selling price is illustrative, not a quotation recommendation.

Cost buffet dishes in batches

For a batch recipe, record the quantity used and purchased cost for each ingredient, then the cooked yield and portions. Cost sauces, dressings, condiments and garnishes as well as the main ingredients. For multiple dishes, aggregate the batch costs before dividing by realistic covers.

Pricing a catering event beyond food ingredients

Separate ingredient costing from the full event quotation. Include staff hours, setup, service, delivery, disposables or hire costs, waste allowance and the appropriate VAT treatment. Food-cost GP alone does not indicate overall event profitability.

What if the guest count changes?

Review recipe batch sizes, purchasing pack formats and yields. Scaling portion counts proportionally can produce different purchase quantities and waste, so check each ingredient instead of assuming costs scale exactly.
